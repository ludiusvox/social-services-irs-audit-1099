# Privacy Policy for LedgerLens

**Effective Date**: October 30, 2026  
**Application Name**: LedgerLens (`com.ledgerlens.app`)  
**License**: MIT License  

---

## 1. Overview
LedgerLens ("the Application") is an offline-first, mobile tax ledger and audit engine designed for 1099 independent contractors, gig workers, and benefit recipients. Your privacy is paramount. LedgerLens does not sell, rent, track, or collect your personal or financial information on external servers.

---

## 2. Information Storage & Collection

### On-Device Local Storage
- All financial records, Schedule C line items, tax safe-harbor calculations, receipt images, and audit verification data are stored **locally on your device** using embedded NoSQL persistent database technology (Dexie.js / IndexedDB).
- We do not operate external database servers that receive or store your financial ledger entries.

---

## 3. Data Sharing Restrictions

LedgerLens operates under a **Strict Non-Sharing Policy**:

- **No Automatic External Transmission**: Your data never leaves your device automatically.
- **User-Initiated Sharing (Text & Email Only)**: Data, reports, or PDF tax verification packages are transmitted **only when explicitly initiated by you** through standard system dialogs (e.g., sending an export or receipt via **SMS/Text** or **Email**).
- **No Third-Party Brokers or Analytics**: We do not share, sell, or disclose your data to third-party ad networks, data brokers, or telemetry services.

---

## 4. Device Permissions

LedgerLens requests only minimal device permissions required for core utility:
- **Camera / Storage Access**: Used exclusively for capturing and saving receipt photos to your local receipt vault.
- **Internet Permission**: Used solely for standard web runtime components and user-initiated email/text file sharing interfaces.
